# Content editor guide

For the Melanated Online team. Sign in at `https://<your-domain>/admin`.

There are two roles. **Admins** (the owner) can do everything. **Authors** (contributing writers) write and edit their own posts; an admin publishes them unless Site Settings says otherwise. Where a step is for one role only, it says so.

## Writing a post

1. **Posts → Create new.** Your work saves automatically as you type (a draft only you and admins can see).
2. Fill in the **title** and a short **excerpt**: one or two sentences, shown on cards and in Google.
3. Add a **cover photo** (required) and describe it in **alt text**: say what's in the picture, for readers who can't see it.
4. Write in **Content**. Use _Heading 2_ for sections, _Quote_ for pull quotes, the image button for a single photo, and the **blocks** button (the + menu) → **Gallery** for a set of photos.
5. In the sidebar, choose the **category** (section) and set the **Disclosure** (see below).
6. Press **Preview** to see the post exactly as readers will, before it goes live.

The **SEO** tab is optional. Leave it blank and the title, excerpt and cover photo are used.

## Disclosures (required by law)

UK advertising rules (ASA and CMA) say readers must know a post is advertising **before** they read it. Set **Disclosure** on every post:

| Choose                    | When                                                                            |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **None**                  | Nothing was paid for, gifted or linked for commission                           |
| **Ad (paid partnership)** | A brand paid for or controlled the post. Add the brand name                     |
| **Ad (affiliate links)**  | The post has links that earn a commission                                       |
| **Gifted**                | A brand sent free products and had no control over the post. Add the brand name |

The label then appears above the title and on the post's card. If in doubt, ask an admin; getting it wrong is a legal and trust risk.

## Photos

- **Cover photos:** landscape, at least 1600px wide. Photos straight from a phone are fine (up to 10 MB); the site makes all the smaller versions itself.
- **Focal point:** when uploading, click the face or subject so it stays in frame when the photo is cropped for cards.
- **Galleries:** 2 to 12 photos, shown 2 or 3 across with an optional caption. Readers can tap a photo to see it full screen. Each photo keeps its own alt text.
- **Credits:** if a photo isn't yours, put the photographer in the image's **Credit** field. Only use photos you own or have permission to use.
- Authors can edit or replace only the photos they uploaded.

## Publishing

- **Admins:** press **Publish** to go live within seconds, or **Schedule publish** to pick a date. Scheduled posts go live at the **next morning release, between about 6 and 7am UK time in winter and 7 and 8am in summer**, not at the exact minute picked. To go live at a precise time, press **Publish** yourself.
- **Authors:** you won't see Publish or Schedule unless an admin has turned on _Authors can publish without review_ (Site Settings → Editorial). Finish your draft and tell an admin it's ready.
- **Featured** (the big story on the home page) and **Trending** (the sidebar list) are set by **admins** only.
- **Related posts** (sidebar): pick up to three. Only published posts are ever shown to readers; if none are picked, the section's latest are used.

## Changing a web address

You can change a post's **slug** or move it to another section after it's live: the old address keeps working and forwards readers to the new one. A **section's** web address can't change once it has published posts (rename the section's title instead).

## Admin-only areas

- **Pages:** standalone pages such as the privacy policy and terms. Edit them like a post, save drafts while you work, and **Publish** when ready. Tick **Show in footer** to link a page from the footer, and update **Last updated** whenever the content changes materially. A page's address can't match a section's.
- **Page content:** the words on the home page (hero button, section headings, the community block) and the Community page. The layout and colours stay fixed, so you can't break the design. Button links must be pages on this site, such as `/community`, or `#latest` to jump to the latest stories.
- **Site Settings:** contact email, social links, and whether authors can publish without review.
- **Insights → Reactions:** how many hearts each post has. A rough sign of what readers enjoy, not an exact measure.
- **Redirects:** created automatically when addresses change; you rarely need to touch them.

## Accounts and passwords

Admins add writers under **Users** with the role **Author**. Each person should have their own login.

There's **no password-reset email yet**. After 5 wrong passwords an account locks for 10 minutes, then you can try again. If you forget your password, ask an admin: they can set a new one for you under **Users**. If the only admin is locked out, contact the developer.
