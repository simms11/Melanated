# Content editor guide

For the Melanated Online editorial team. Log in at `https://<your-domain>/admin`.

## Publishing a post

1. **Posts → Create new.**
2. Fill in the **title**, a short **excerpt** (this is what shows on cards and in Google), and upload a **hero image**. Add **alt text** describing the image — it's required.
3. Write in the **Content** editor. Use _Heading 2_ for section headings, _Quote_ for pull quotes, and the image button to add photos inline.
4. In the sidebar, choose the **category** and check **Featured** to make it the big story on the home page or **Trending** to pin it in the sidebar list.
5. **Save draft** to keep working, or **Publish** to go live. Use **Schedule publish** to set a date. Scheduled posts go live **at the next morning release, around 6 to 7am UK time**, not at the exact minute you pick. To publish at a precise time, press **Publish** yourself. **Authors:** scheduling only works if an admin has turned on _Authors can publish without review_. Otherwise a scheduled post stays a draft, so save your draft and ask an admin to schedule it.
6. The page is live within seconds — no need to ask the developer.

The **SEO** tab is optional. Leave it blank and the title, excerpt and hero image are used.

## Images

- Use landscape photos at least 1600px wide for hero images; portrait (4:5) crops look best on cards.
- The system automatically creates optimised versions — upload the best quality you have.
- Set a **focal point** on upload if a face should stay in frame when cropped.

## Site settings

**Site Settings** (admins only) controls the contact email, WhatsApp link, social links, newsletter form and whether authors can publish without review.

## Accounts

Admins can add writers under **Users**. Choose the role **Author** for contributors. Authors can edit only their own posts, can't see other writers' unpublished drafts, and can only edit or replace images they uploaded themselves. Admins can do everything.
